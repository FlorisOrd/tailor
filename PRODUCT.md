## Product Owner Authority

The product owner decides matters that materially affect:

- product behavior, priorities, and acceptance outcomes;
- UX direction, content, visual identity, and branding;
- business model, spending, vendors, and external commitments;
- collection or use of personal data;
- privacy, legal, regulatory, and compliance obligations;
- irreversible actions, including public launches and destructive migrations.

Agents should resolve routine engineering choices autonomously and present owner decisions in plain language with a recommendation and meaningful tradeoffs.

## Requirements Template

Before implementation, Product / Requirements records:

1. **Problem and outcome** — who benefits and what should improve.
2. **Scope** — included and explicitly excluded behavior.
3. **Acceptance criteria** — observable, testable outcomes.
4. **UX states** — happy path, loading, empty, error, and recovery states where relevant.
5. **Constraints** — privacy, security, accessibility, legal, budget, compatibility, and deadline needs.
6. **Success evidence** — tests, browser observations, or measurable results that demonstrate completion.

Requirements changes after implementation starts must be documented and re-evaluated for architecture, security, QA, and release impact.
